How to cut and fold
  1. Print one-sided in landscape at 100% scale with headers and footers off.
  2. Crease all the panel boundaries, then unfold the sheet.
  3. Cut along the middle horizontal crease, between the first and third vertical creases.
  4. Fold along the long center crease with the printed sides facing out. Push the short ends together until the slit opens into a cross.
  5. Wrap the pages around the cross with page 1 facing out.
